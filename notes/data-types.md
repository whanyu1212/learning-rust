# Data Types

> **TL;DR** — Start with scalars and `String` vs `&str`, then collections and references, then `Option`/`Result` and custom types. Ownership shows up as soon as you meet `&str`.

Three pages, in order:

1. [Scalars & Strings](./rust-data-types.md) — integers, floats, bools, chars, tuples, arrays, `String` vs `&str`.
2. [Collections & Refs](./rust-collections.md) — `Vec`, `HashMap`, `HashSet`, `&T`/`&mut T`, functions, closures.
3. [Option, Result & Custom](./rust-custom-types.md) — null-safety, error values, structs, enums, smart pointers.

After this, [Ownership](./rust-ownership.md) explains why the owned-vs-borrowed split exists.
